Entry for the [Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046) on make.fun — write one short, funny rant (1–400 characters) about a small everyday annoyance, and let a furious judge pick the one that lands hardest.

## The rant

> One wheel on every supermarket trolley has chosen chaos. It spins sideways, squeaks like a haunted gate, and drags me into the biscuit aisle. I came for milk. I left pushing a trolley with opinions — and no milk.

212 characters. No printer, no self-checkout, no 3 a.m. smoke detector — just the trolley wheel that steers itself into the biscuits while squeaking like a haunted gate.

## Why this annoyance

Everyone has pushed that trolley: three wheels glide, the fourth has a
personality disorder. It never squeaks on the way in — only once you are
committed, halfway down an aisle, with a full basket and an audience. The
rant stays concrete (one wheel, one aisle, one missing bottle of milk) and
lets the trolley do the talking: it is the only thing in the supermarket
with opinions, and it uses them all on the biscuit aisle.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the rant text to `/output/entry.txt` when run:

```sh
node main.ts
```

It creates `/output` if needed, writes the rant as UTF-8, and prints the character count. Nothing is read from the network, the environment, or anywhere outside this repository.
